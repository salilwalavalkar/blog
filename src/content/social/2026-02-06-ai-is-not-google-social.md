## 🐦 X / Twitter

Best AI tip I heard this year: type "push back" into every prompt. Because the default setting is flattery, and apparently some of us like it that way. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-02-06-ai-is-not-google/

## 🧵 Threads

Watched a 40-minute interview with NYU Stern's Chief AI Architect. The good advice fits on a sticky note. Write your own first draft. Then ask "what am I missing?" five times in a row. Stop treating the chat box like Google.

The bit that stuck with me is the other part. AI "doesn't know what quality looks like," so we still need people with taste. Also it's eating the entry-level jobs where people used to get that taste. His answer, to his credit: "I'm not sure how to solve that problem."

Me neither. But someone should, before the last senior retires. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-02-06-ai-is-not-google/

## 🐘 Mastodon

Notes on Conor Grennan on Silicon Valley Girl. The useful bits: treat the model as a process tool, not an answer engine (break a decision into steps and work each one), write your own first draft, and verify important stuff against the real source, not a second chatbot. The weak spot is that "AI can't judge quality, so humans stay" runs straight into "AI does the junior work where humans learn to judge quality." Nobody on the show has an answer for that yet. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-02-06-ai-is-not-google/
