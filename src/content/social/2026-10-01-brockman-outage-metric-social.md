## 🧵 Threads

Greg Brockman, on how OpenAI knows AI is making people productive: when the models go down internally, everyone says they can't work. Six months ago they didn't have it. Now two hours without it is a crisis. He thinks that's great.

Better still, the same YouTube channel posted him defending token leaderboards as "a good proxy metric." The next day it posted Airbnb's CEO saying token leaderboards were "clearly" not going to work long term. One of them sells tokens.

The rule-of-five automation advice is good though. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-10-01-brockman-outage-metric/

## 🐘 Mastodon

Notes on Greg Brockman on Silicon Valley Girl. Good: automate a task only after you've done it by hand five times, and build products that improve when models improve, not ones that patch around current model limits. Bad: people not being able to work during a two-hour model outage, offered as proof of productivity. That's a hard dependency with no fallback. Also bad: an agent querying the data warehouse that used to take a data team a week, with no mention of who reviews the SQL. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-10-01-brockman-outage-metric/
