## 🐦 X / Twitter

The studio behind work for Slack and Uber says the secret to delightful software is a boring stack and an ugly first version full of raw JSON. Perfectionism is just waterfall with better fonts. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-07-01-building-software-people-love/

## 🧵 Threads

Metalab's VP of Engineering on how they build software people love. Spoiler: they spend more time talking clients _out_ of delightful features than into them.

Their first versions are "ugly apps": the whole flow end to end, with some screens that just render JSON and a button. Clients get confused by the curly brackets, then start reordering screens and fixing the flow before anyone picks a font.

Also: they built custom AI tooling, then threw it out when the vendors shipped better versions. Welcome to the frontier. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-07-01-building-software-people-love/

## 🐘 Mastodon

Notes on SE Daily's episode with Wesley Yu of Metalab. Stack choice starts with what the client can maintain and hire for, not what's new: "a stack decision is not just a technical exercise. It's also a hiring plan." New tech goes in isolated sidecars. First builds are ugly apps that are complete flows with raw JSON screens, refined iteratively. And their custom LLM eval tools and MCP servers got binned once vendors caught up. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-07-01-building-software-people-love/
