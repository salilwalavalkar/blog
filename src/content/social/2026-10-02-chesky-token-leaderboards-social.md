## 🐦 X / Twitter

Airbnb's CEO admits token leaderboards were a bad idea. The replacement metric is "we ship 80% more features." Same idea, new dashboard. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-10-02-chesky-token-leaderboards/

## 🧵 Threads

Brian Chesky on how companies measured AI adoption this year. First, token leaderboards that shamed people who weren't burning enough tokens. Then, "wait, why are we spending so much money on AI?"

The fix is product velocity. Airbnb ships 80% more features than a year ago. Anyone who's been measured on tickets closed knows how this goes. The tickets just get smaller.

He's dead right about one thing, though. Cramming AI into a chat box is "putting a jet engine on the back of a bicycle." https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-10-02-chesky-token-leaderboards/

## 🐘 Mastodon

Notes on Brian Chesky on Silicon Valley Girl. Good points: a chatbot is a UI and an agent is an app, and they aren't the same thing. Chat is also a poor fit for comparison shopping with three people and twenty filters. The weak point: after dropping token leaderboards, Airbnb measures "product velocity," 80% more features shipped. That's an output count, and output counts get gamed the same way input counts do. The quality metrics he mentions never get named. https://salilwalavalkar.github.io/blog/posts/2026-10-02-chesky-token-leaderboards/
